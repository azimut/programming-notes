- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stepper_motor
- brushless DC electric motor
- given a step position
- don't need a position sensor for feedback

- Driving types:
  - bipolar (4 cables)
    - +torque
    - -maxspeed
    - voltage polarity reversal
  - unipolar (5,6 cables)
    - -torque
    - +maxspeed
    - no voltage polarity reversal
    - needs at least 5 cables
    - 1 extra for a tap to the center of a pair of coils

- Sheet:
  - min delay between steps (eg: 2ms)
  - steps per revolution
  - max speed (steps/sec)
  - Inductance:
    - in millihenries
    - higher means less maxspeed

- Drivers:
  - L/R (aka constant voltage)
  - Chopper

- Microstepping:
  - affects max velocity
  - affects torque
  - smoother rotation
  - quieter
  - < half-step, eg: 1/4
  - uses different currents (needs a driver)
