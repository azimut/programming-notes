- servo motor
  + dc motor
  + gearbox: speed-- torque++ (a compound gear train)
  + electonics
    - feedback sensor: potentiometer/magnetometer/rotary encoder
    - pcb: h-bridge, comparator

- 3 cables (+/-/PWM)

- speed can be controlled by breaking down a movement into steps with delays in between
- speed and torque also determined by voltage
- uses PWM to control angle/position
  - a pulse every 20ms aka 50Hz
  - angle: by the duration of the pwm (0.5ms - 2.5ms)

- types: micro & standard

- by control system
  - open loop: 360° rotation/travel
  - closed loop: aprox 180° rotation/travel (sometimes 160°)
