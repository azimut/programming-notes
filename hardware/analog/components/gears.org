2 gears can invert the rotation

Backlash: when two gears are apart but touching and wooble
Pulley: for a timing belt
Shaft: engine pin
Boss: lump on the side (for engines)

Materials
- Plastic
- Steel
- Stainless Steel
- Brass
- Phosphorus Bronce ($)

Sizes
- 1 MOD - 62 gears                         - 60mm pitch diameter
- 1 MOD - 82 gears - 82mm outside diameter - 80mm pitch diameter
- 2 MOD - 40mm pitch diameter
- 0.5 MOD - 64 gears

Types:
- Antibacklash: two gears tied together by springs (for instruments)
- Pin Gears: work on angle, slow (for clocks)
- Rack Gear: straight
- Worm Gear: 
  - made like from a threaded rod
  - tilted against other gears
  - for light loads
  - great reduction
  - other gears cannot move it (self locking)
- ring/planetary
  - can be stacked
  - good reduction (movement source is the shaft)
  - for wind turbines (disc is movement source)
- bevel gears: 
  - to rotate 90° the rotation
  - small load
- angle/helical gears
  - more contact area
  - more load
  - quieter/less vibration
  - cons: some sliding, push appart
  - change 90° rotation
  - eg: for hand disc cutters, cars (differential, gearbox)
- herringbone
  - v-shape, cancel sideways force of bevel gears
- differential gears:
  - on cars
  - for calculations (!?) in early "computers"
